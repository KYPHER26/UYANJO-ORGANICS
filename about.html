Our story

Made by hand, in small batches, in Njiro.

Uyanjo Organics started as a simple idea — that hair and skin care should be made from things you could name. Every jar is still blended, poured and labelled by hand in our Arusha workshop.

What "organic" means to us

We build every product around a short list of ingredients we trust: bee wax, castor oil, coconut oil, turmeric and honey, along with a touch of micro wax and fragrance for finish. Nothing goes into a jar we wouldn't explain to a customer standing in front of us.

That same standard carries across our full range — hair growth grease, chebe treatments, shampoo, conditioner, shower gel, and the guest soaps we supply to hotels, camps and lodges around Arusha.

Rooted in Arusha

Working out of Njiro, we're close to the ingredients and the community we serve. Many of our regulars first found us through word of mouth — a jar passed from one friend, one salon, one household to the next.

  • Small-batch, hand-poured production
  • Ingredients we can name and explain
  • Trusted by home users, salons and hospitality partners
